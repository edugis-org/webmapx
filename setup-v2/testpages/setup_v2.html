    Map

    Stories

    Guided tours through the map, in chapters and steps.

    The Stories tool is off. Viewers can only open a story from the Stories button in a toolbar, so turn the tool on before writing one.

    1. Story Click the name to rename it; the gear holds its other settings.

    No stories yet — click "+ New story" to create one.

    2. Outline Click a step to show it on the map, click a title to rename it. Drag ⠿ to reorder.

    3. Set the map view for this step Where the map looks during this step.

    More: exact numbers, projection, 3D terrain

    4. Layers in this step From the Map catalog. Top of the list is on top of the map; the eye hides a layer without removing it.

    5. Edit step Edit the title and content of this step.
